# Safeer Mobile 1.0.27

**Downloads inside Safeer, a better remote.**

- Menu → Downloads now opens a list inside Safeer (files you downloaded and files received over Safeer Link, by day, with progress for running downloads): tap to open — PDFs in Safeer's own viewer — long-press for Share or Delete. No more jumping to the system app you could not return from. A received file's notification opens this list.
- Remote: the Menu key opens the menu on the TV (or phone) and the remote's keys then move inside it; the header says which device is the Safeer Link hub and whose apps are listed; app entries without a real name are gone.
- Safeer Link: the home page is no longer offered for sending; a refused action does not colour the window yellow; a pairing the hub no longer knows leads to a new pairing instead of endless connecting.
- Hub: a sibling device on the same computer (Safeer Control next to Safeer Browser) can join without a new code.

Slovensko: Meni → Prenosi zdaj odpre seznam v Safeerju (prenesene in prek Safeer Linka prejete datoteke, po dnevih, z napredkom): dotik odpre – PDF v Safeerjevem pregledovalniku – dolg pritisk ponudi Deli ali Izbriši. Nič več skoka v sistemsko aplikacijo, iz katere se ni bilo mogoče vrniti. Daljinec: tipka Meni odpre meni na televizorju, tipke se nato premikajo po njem; glava pove središče Safeer Linka in čigave aplikacije kaže. Safeer Link: domače strani ne ponujamo za pošiljanje; seznanitev, ki je središče ne pozna več, vodi v novo seznanitev. Središče: sorodna naprava na istem računalniku (Safeer Control ob Safeer Browserju) vstopi brez nove kode.
