# Safeer Mobile 1.0.31 → 1.0.32

**Your TV can rotate and delete the photos on your phone.** In Safeer OS on the TV: Files → your phone → Pictures → hold OK → *Rotate left / right* or *Delete*.

- **Android asks you first.** A photo the phone did not create itself may only be changed with your consent, so the phone shows the system question (“Allow Safeer Mobile Browser to modify this photo?”). The TV waits for your answer and shows the result by itself — the photo turns or disappears on the TV as soon as you tap *Allow*. If you tap *Deny*, the TV says so. For the same photo you are asked only once.
- **Delete = Trash.** The photo goes to the phone's Trash, where you can restore it.
- **Rotating a JPEG does not touch a single pixel** — only the EXIF orientation changes, instantly. Other images (PNG, WebP) are rotated and saved again.
- **Notifications, asked once.** The question has to reach you even when Safeer is in the background (Android no longer lets an app open a window from the background), so the app asks for permission to show notifications on first start. Without it, confirmations — and screen sharing requests — could not appear.

Everything from 1.0.31 is unchanged.

Slovensko: **Televizor zna zavrteti in izbrisati fotografije na tvojem telefonu** (Safeer OS na TV: Datoteke → telefon → Slike → zadrži OK → *Zavrti* ali *Izbriši*). Za fotografijo, ki je telefon ni ustvaril sam, **Android najprej vpraša tebe** – televizor počaka na odgovor in izid pokaže sam; ob zavrnitvi to pošteno pove. Za isto fotografijo vpraša samo enkrat. **Brisanje = Smeti telefona** (lahko jo obnoviš). **JPEG se zavrti brez izgube** (samo oznaka EXIF). Ob prvem zagonu aplikacija **enkrat vpraša za obvestila** – brez njih vprašanje za potrditev (in za deljenje zaslona) ne bi moglo priti do tebe, ko je Safeer v ozadju. Vse iz 1.0.31 ostaja.
