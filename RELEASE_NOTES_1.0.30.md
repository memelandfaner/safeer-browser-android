# Safeer Mobile 1.0.30

**Your phone hosts Safeer Link for everyone – and every device can be named.**

- **Safeer Link Web on the phone hub.** When your phone is the Safeer Link hub, *Connect a new device* shows a code that any other phone opens in its browser – remote control, links and text without installing anything (home network only).
- **Name your devices.** Rename any device (including this phone) on the Safeer Link page; the name is kept by the hub and shown the same on every TV, tablet and computer.
- **Your videos, music and pictures for other devices.** Turn on *Videos, music and pictures* on the Safeer Link page and the TV or tablet lists them in Safeer OS Files (direct, encrypted connection).
- **Computer sound on the phone** (audio.play) and hub invitations/leaving.
- **Join a TV with the QR code from the Safeer OS sign-in window**, and confirm a computer's sign-in (Allow) on the phone.
- **Safeer Link core:** device key (circle of trust – no new code after a hub change), automatic hub election, session tokens, the phone as an app provider (apps.list / apps.launch), one shared source with the TV.

Slovensko: **Safeer Link Web na telefonu kot središču** – *Poveži novo napravo* pokaže kodo, ki jo drug telefon odpre v brskalniku: daljinec, povezave in besedilo brez namestitve (samo domače omrežje). **Poimenuj naprave** (tudi ta telefon) na strani Safeer Link; ime hrani središče in ga vse naprave kažejo enako. **Videi, glasba in slike za druge naprave:** vklop na strani Safeer Link, TV ali tablica jih pokaže v Datotekah Safeer OS. **Zvok računalnika na telefonu**, vabila in odhod iz središča. **Pridružitev televizorju s QR kodo** iz prijavnega okna Safeer OS in potrditev prijave računalnika (Dovoli) na telefonu. **Jedro Safeer Link:** ključ naprave (krog zaupanja), samodejna izvolitev središča, sejni žetoni, telefon kot ponudnik aplikacij, en vir s TV.
