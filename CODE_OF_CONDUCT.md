# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the person.

## What we expect

- Welcoming and patient behaviour towards people of every background and level of experience.
- Disagreement argued on technical merit, with reasons.
- Accepting a maintainer's decision gracefully once it has been explained, and saying so
  plainly when you think it is wrong.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the
  project meets.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately wasting maintainers' time.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages —
and when you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the **Security** tab's private reporting,
or by opening an issue if the matter is not sensitive. Reports are handled discreetly.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may block
repeat offenders. We would much rather explain once than block anyone.
